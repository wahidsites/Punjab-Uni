How to apply

Four steps from first look to your seat.

  1. Pick a program

    Check the program list and its eligibility.

  2. Send your request

    Fill the form below with your name, email and program.

  3. Get a reply

    The admissions team contacts you by email.

  4. Complete the official process

    Final registration and merit lists run on the official site.

Eligibility

  • Undergraduate: Intermediate (12 years of education) or equivalent
  • Master's: relevant bachelor's degree (14 to 16 years)
  • PhD: relevant MPhil/MS degree (18 years)
  • Some programs add an entry test or interview

Key dates

  • Application windows differ for each session and program
  • Entry test and merit list dates are announced by the university
  • Check pu.edu.pk for the current schedule
